# Standard Human NPC Armor, Detectors, Grenades and NVG Generator Audit

**Source:** STALKER 2 runtime/game + DLC CFG dump 2.0.5  
**Scope:** standard-human NPC loadout armor/head/detector/internal-`Artifact` rows plus directly nested armor/NVG/relevant pools  
**Status:** deep-audited; no runtime writer changes made

## Verdict

The standard-human equipment surface contains **129 direct relevant rows across 40 externally used standard-human loadout generators**: **43 BodyArmor**, **6 Head**, **36 Detector**, and **44 internal `Artifact` rows**. The internal `Artifact` rows are not anomaly artifacts in this domain: every one of them is either `GrenadeRGD5` or `GrenadeF1`.

There is also one standard-NPC-only internal armor pool, `GeneralNPC_MIK_Armor`, with **9 BodyArmor rows**, bringing the reviewed standard-domain item-row total to **138** before counting shared nested definitions.

As with weapons, the externally used standard-human loadout generators themselves are structurally isolated: their impact context is `human_npc_standard` and they have no incoming composition/inheritance references. The important difference is that much of armor and all reviewed NVG generation is reached through **nested pools**, many of which are reused by named NPCs, zombies, quests or corpse overrides. Pool definitions therefore cannot be treated as standard-NPC-only just because a standard loadout points at them.

## Semantic split

| Gameplay surface | Runtime representation | Structural result | Control-design result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Detector | 36 direct `Echo` rows in standard loadouts | **clean direct allowlist** | Strong structural candidate for a detector-generation chance control; runtime behavior still needs in-game proof. |
| Grenades | 44 direct rows under CFG category `Artifact` | direct rows are standard-NPC-only | Not one uniform chance surface: 36 active explicit-`Chance`, 6 disabled `Chance=0`, and 2 `Weight`-only rows. |
| Direct armor / helmet | 43 BodyArmor + 6 Head rows | direct rows are standard-NPC-only | Mostly relative `Weight` selection, not a global drop chance; direct neutral/SIRCAA rows are scope-safe but semantics are selection-distribution semantics. |

| MIK armor | `GeneralNPC_MIK_Armor` (9 rows) | standard-NPC-only internal pool used by four MIK loadouts | Scope-safe for MIK-only changes; still Weight-based selection rather than a 1:1 drop-chance scalar. |
| NVG | nested `GeneralNPC_Freedom_NVG` / `GeneralNPC_Neutral_NVG` | shared across other NPC/quest/zombie contexts | Pool edits excluded. Standard-loadout source edges are isolated but represent whole-pool inclusion, not per-NVG chance semantics. |
| Actual anomaly artifacts | none found as direct standard-human item rows in this audited surface | no direct standard-human target | **No artifact-drop control should be inferred from the enum name `Artifact`.** In these loadouts that enum is grenade generation. |

## Detector findings

All **36** direct detector rows generate only `Echo`. **30** use `Chance=0.2` and **6** use `Chance=0.05`; all have `MinCount=1`. These rows are inside isolated standard-human loadout generators and do not require editing a shared detector pool.

That makes detector generation the cleanest control candidate in this audit. It is still only **CFG/writer structurally provable** until an in-game test confirms how corpse/inventory persistence and drop behavior expose the generated detector.

## Grenade findings — CFG `Artifact` is not anomaly-artifact loot here

The standard-human loadouts contain **44** grenade rows: **29 `GrenadeRGD5`** and **15 `GrenadeF1`**. No anomaly-artifact SID appears in these direct rows.

Of those grenade rows, **36** have an active explicit `Chance`, **6** are Prolog rows with `Chance=0`, and **2** use `Weight` instead of `Chance`. Therefore a single “NPC Grenade Drop Chance” slider over all rows would still be an aggregate policy rather than a 1:1 field mapping.

Some grenade rows also carry durability/ammo-shaped fields in the source CFG. Those fields are recorded verbatim below but are **not assigned gameplay meaning here**; they should not be interpreted as grenade condition or ammunition without runtime evidence.

## Armor and helmet findings

Direct standard-human loadouts contain **43 BodyArmor rows across 6 loadouts** and **6 Head rows across 5 loadouts**. BodyArmor selection is entirely Weight-based in these direct rows. Head selection is mixed (`Chance` on most rows, `Weight` on one), so there is no uniform armor/helmet drop-chance field.

The majority of faction armor is not defined directly in the standard loadout. Instead the loadout links to a faction armor sub-generator. Those source links are standard-NPC-only, but the target pool definitions are frequently reused elsewhere. Editing the shared armor pool changes every consumer, not just the standard loadout that led us to it.

`GeneralNPC_MIK_Armor` is the important exception: it has no external runtime consumer of its own and is composed only by the four standard MIK loadouts. Its **9** BodyArmor rows are therefore scope-safe for MIK-only research, but still use relative `Weight` values rather than a direct probability scalar.

## NVG findings

No standard-human loadout in this audited group contains a direct `NightVision` item row. Instead, five standard-loadout links reach two shared NVG pools: four MIK loadouts point at `GeneralNPC_Freedom_NVG`, while `GeneralNPC_Neutral_Sniper_ItemGeneratorCustom` points at `GeneralNPC_Neutral_NVG`. Both pool definitions have non-standard consumers, so their contents are excluded from standard-NPC-only pool edits.

## Control-safety decisions

| Candidate idea | Exact source surface | Standard-NPC-only? | 1:1 field mapping? | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard NPC detector generation chance | 36 direct `Echo` `Chance` rows | Yes | **Yes, structurally** | **Best candidate from this domain.** Runtime-test before calling gameplay behavior confirmed. |
| Standard NPC grenade chance | 44 direct grenade rows | Yes | **No across all rows** | **NO UI.** 36 active Chance rows are clean; 6 Prologue Chance=0 rows remain disabled; 2 Weight-only rows are normal repeatable Varta Stormtrooper / Noon Recon loadouts, so an all-NPC percentage would be incomplete. |
| Standard NPC armor drop chance | direct armor rows + nested armor edges/pools | Mixed | No | **Do not implement as one chance slider.** Most armor is relative Weight selection and many pool definitions are shared. |
| Standard NPC armor selection weights | exact direct armor rows | Yes for direct rows | Yes as Weight fields, but relative semantics | Possible advanced/research surface, not a normalized drop-chance control. |
| MIK armor selection | `GeneralNPC_MIK_Armor` 9 rows | Yes, for four MIK loadouts | Yes as Weight fields | Structurally safe MIK-only selection surface; not a global NPC armor scalar. |
| Standard NPC helmet chance | six direct Head rows plus shared faction armor pools | Mixed | No globally | Direct neutral subset is isolated, but all-faction helmet tuning would require shared-pool handling. |
| Standard NPC NVG chance | five source edges into two shared NVG pools | Edge yes, pool no | No per item | Pool definitions excluded; source-edge semantics require runtime proof before any bundle control. |
| Standard NPC anomaly-artifact chance | no direct anomaly-artifact rows found | — | — | **Do not implement from the `Artifact` enum.** In this domain the enum maps to grenades. |

## Audit decision

- **Detector direct rows:** structurally approved as a future allowlist candidate; gameplay semantics still need runtime validation.
- **Grenade direct rows:** fully mapped, but **not approved as one uniform all-grenade Chance control** because the source mixes active Chance, disabled Chance=0 and Weight-only entries.
- **Direct armor/head rows:** scope-safe, but mostly selection-weight semantics rather than drop-chance semantics.
- **Shared faction armor/NVG pool definitions:** **excluded** from standard-NPC-only controls.
- **`GeneralNPC_MIK_Armor`:** standard-NPC-only internal pool; safe scope, narrow MIK semantics.
- **`GamePass_Stash_ItemGenerator_Rare`:** explicitly excluded as an equipment target; its contents cross ammo/consumable/grenade/money domains and it is shared with world-container use.
- **Actual anomaly artifacts:** no direct standard-human target found in this audited surface.
